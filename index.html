03Fram

One risk layer. Three horizons.

What is possible depends on the vessel, the ice, and the window. Fram quantifies ice risk for a given vessel class across operational, seasonal and retrospective timescales.

Hours to days

Nowcast

Ice risk for vessels underway. Current conditions, near-term change, and the routes that remain open for a given ice class.

Weeks to months

Seasonal

Outlooks of ice conditions and access windows, with uncertainty, to support scheduling, chartering and mission planning.

Past seasons

Hindcast

Retrospective assessment of routes and decisions. What the ice did, what alternatives existed, and the fuel, cost and risk implications.
